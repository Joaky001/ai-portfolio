## Why Portugal

- Spanish speaker → Portuguese accessible within one semester
- Dramatically cheaper than US/UK without sacrificing quality
- Lisbon tech scene is real: Google, Amazon, Microsoft all expanding there
- Post-graduation residency path exists — can stay, work, and build a company
- IST graduates: 97.7% employed, avg €1,839/month net, 86% within 6 months of graduating

Portugal isn't a fallback. It's a strategic entry point into Europe.

## Career Target

**AI Security** — the intersection of adversarial machine learning, prompt injection, model robustness, and cybersecurity. One of the least crowded and most high-value niches in tech right now.

Entry-level cybersecurity in Portugal starts around €37,000/year. AI/ML specialists average €65,200/year. Senior roles reach €117,000+. Both fields growing — cybersecurity at 8%/year in Portugal, AI/ML at 15%/year globally.

The path: **CS degree → master's in Security or AI → build experience in Portugal/Europe → leverage internationally (US, UK, Germany, Netherlands) if desired.**
